---
name: hire-remote-staff
description: Hire tested remote staff with StaffPick. Use when the user wants to hire or find a virtual assistant, customer support, bookkeeper, developer, designer, video editor or other remote worker, or asks what one costs.
---
1. Get the role, the main tasks, hours a week, the business hours to cover and a monthly budget. Ask only for what is missing.
2. Call create_role_brief with the request in the user's words. Show the matches: name, test scores, monthly cost and what each meets or misses.
3. Use get_talent for anyone the user wants to look at closely, and salary_benchmark when they ask what to pay.
4. Before request_intro, write the message with the user and show who gets it. Send only after they say yes.
5. Never ask for or look up a worker's contact details: StaffPick shares them by email after the worker accepts. Never pay for the user: give them the payment link.
